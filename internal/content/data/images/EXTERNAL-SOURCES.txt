External image sources

Every other image in this directory is extracted from the Study Guide PDF
itself (see cmd/ingest/curated.go). The four files below are the sole
exception: genuinely public-domain reference maps sourced from outside the
four supplied PDFs, added because Chapters 1, 6, and 7's own map graphics
carry no printed credit and so cannot be reproduced under this project's
image policy. Each was individually verified against its source page before
being downloaded; none came from a general "fair use" image search. Re-verify
against the URL below if a file is ever regenerated or its credit questioned.

ext-usgs-reference-map.jpg
  Title: "General Reference Printable Map", The National Atlas of the
    United States of America (map itself is dated 2003, Reston, Virginia)
  Source page: https://www.usgs.gov/media/images/general-reference-printable-map
  Direct image URL (as downloaded): https://d9-wret.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/assets/palladium/production/s3fs-public/printable2.png
  Rights statement quoted from the source page: "Public Domain."
  Credit used in manifest.json: "Courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey."
  Verified: 2026-09-20
  Coverage note: shows all 50 states, D.C., Canada/Mexico borders, the
    Pacific/Atlantic oceans and Gulf of Mexico, the Mississippi and Missouri
    rivers, and the Rocky and Appalachian mountains (labeled on the map
    itself). Does NOT show the five U.S. territories — Chapter 6's
    territory-related text transcriptions are unaffected by this image.

Two categories were researched and explicitly rejected for lack of a
verifiable public-domain source, and remain text transcriptions:
  - A map of the five U.S. territories with their capital cities marked.
  - A historical map showing both the European colonization routes to
    Jamestown/Plymouth and the transatlantic slave-trade routes (Chapter 7,
    page 45). Library of Congress item pages, which held the most promising
    candidates for this one, returned HTTP 403 (Cloudflare bot protection) to
    both WebFetch and curl and could not be verified.
